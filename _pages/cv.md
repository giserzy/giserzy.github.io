Research Interests
======
My research focuses on **GeoAI, multimodal representation learning, and social sensing** for **urban environment modeling, human mobility, environmental exposure, and urban big data services**.

* **GeoAI and multimodal representation learning:** Developing foundation models and deep learning methods for multimodal geospatial data, urban environment modeling, and transferable built-environment representations.
* **Human mobility with spatiotemporal big data:** Using social media, mobile sensing, and trajectory data to reconstruct individual travel trajectories and activity patterns, and to reveal mobility inequality and dynamic environmental exposure in high-density cities.
* **Urban big data services and products:** Translating research outputs into datasets, open-source tools, and decision-support systems for urban planning, environmental justice, and disaster response.
